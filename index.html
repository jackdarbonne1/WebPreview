The Cause

The Device

Designed for the
point of care.

Lumenate's catheter-based cystoscope is being designed for non-urologists. It is designed to capture the images urologists need — without a urologist in the room, and without taking them out of the loop.

Our Approach

Enabling providers already in every community to deliver the screening patients need.

Lumenate Medical aims to enable non-urologist providers — Physician Associates and Nurse Practitioners — to perform bladder cancer screening, with specialist oversight built in. Multiplying what the existing urology workforce can reach.

Step 01
Image collection by non-urologist providers, such as PAs & NPs
Community providers use Lumenate's device to capture cystoscopy images with minimal training and built-in guidance.
Step 02
Asynchronous image review by urologists
Board-certified urologists review images remotely on their schedule — no patient visit required.
Impact
Faster results, earlier diagnosis
The goal: patients receive results in days, not months.
For Urologists

Helping more patients without adding hours to your day

Lumenate is designed to extend your reach — multiplying what you can oversee without adding hours to your day. You stay the decision-maker on every case, while routine diagnostics stop competing with the patients who need you most.

Task-shifting routine procedures to reclaim specialist time
Expanding throughput within the existing workforce.
Refocusing on the higher-complexity procedures that only urologists can do
Procedures like tumor resections and stone surgeries.
Simplifying screening to improve care everywhere
Time freed goes toward new patients — accelerating all urologic care.
Impact

Better outcomes for everyone in the system.

Task-shifting routine cystoscopy creates capacity across the entire practice — not just the cancer pathway. Earlier diagnosis and expanded access are projected to create measurable value for patients, providers, practices, and payers alike.
